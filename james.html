What Is the James Smith TDEE Calculator?

The James Smith TDEE Calculator is a simple yet powerful tool that helps you estimate how many calories your body burns in a full day. This value is known as your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

If your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, or weight maintenance, knowing your TDEE is essential. You can try the official version here: James Smith Tdee Calculator

How Does It Work?

The calculator first estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), which is the number of calories your body burns at rest. It then multiplies this number by your activity level to calculate your TDEE.

What Is TDEE?

  • BMR: Calories burned at rest
  • Activity Calories: Movement, workouts, daily tasks
  • TDEE: Total calories burned per day

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Enter your age, height, and weight
  2. Select your gender
  3. Choose your real activity level
  4. Click Calculate TDEE

How to Use Your Result

  • Maintain weight: Eat around your TDEE
  • Lose fat: Eat fewer calories than TDEE
  • Gain muscle: Eat more calories than TDEE

For best accuracy, recalculate your TDEE whenever your body weight or training routine changes.
